Hunt mode — working prototype
Tap the gold + to run the flow: search a species, pick a game, pick a method, start. The sheet grows step by step and the chips at its top jump back.

On each hunt card, ×N cycles the step (1 · 2 · 3 · 5 · 10) for hordes and chains; − always corrects by exactly 1. The ✦ button marks it found — behind a confirm, which is also where you can abandon a hunt. Fresh catches record their real hunt time and an optional nickname. The Games tab toggles the Shiny Charm, which changes the odds shown in the method step.

In Nuzlocke, start a run: pick a game, set the house rules, then log each location's first encounter from its known pool — status, nickname, dupes flagged. Boss cards open a trainer sheet with movesets and a Beaten toggle. Caught Pokémon take a nature from the built-in nature guide. Route data is seeded from Platinum for now. End run lives in the header — tap it twice.

Dex is per game — pick the game from the selector (Platinum and Scarlet seeded so far) to change locations, availability, evolutions and movesets. Species are grouped by generation in national-dex order. Tap one for stats, weaknesses and resists from the full type chart. Living and Shiny turn the same grid into a checklist — tap a tile to register it, and the bar counts only what is obtainable in the selected game. A shiny counts for the living dex too, marked with a gold dot; shinies you finish in Hunt register themselves and can't be unticked by hand.

Team mirrors the party of the active run — it has no roster of its own, so with no run it says so and points you at Nuzlocke. Coverage is computed over all 18 types from the party's own types, and a weakness shared by three or more is called out. Check a matchup lists the trainers on the run's timeline and ranks your party against the chosen one; boss sheets in Nuzlocke open the same view directly. Their squads are seeded, so their side is real — yours is type-only, since the prototype holds no levels, stats or movesets for your Pokémon.

Reference is the search button in every header, not a tab. It opens over whatever you were doing and searches moves, abilities, species and the type chart, all built from data already in the app — move entries know which seeded species use them. Species results jump into the Dex.
